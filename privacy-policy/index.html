Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy applies to Clicksmith for Android and explains how it handles information. Clicksmith is a tap-automation utility intended for a general audience, including children. This policy does not cover Clicksmith desktop versions.

Information Clicksmith handles

Clicksmith does not collect or transmit personal information to Scorpionlegend LLC or other companies. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Tap settings, the floating target's position, and your acceptance of the accessibility disclosure are saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. They remain there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Clicksmith.

Accessibility service

If you choose to enable Clicksmith's Accessibility Service, it displays a movable tap target and floating controls over other apps. When you press Start, it sends tap gestures at the target position according to your settings. The service does not read other apps' screen content or send accessibility data to Clicksmith or its servers.

Device backups

If Android device backup is enabled, Android may include Clicksmith's locally stored app preferences in a device backup. Backup availability, handling, and deletion are controlled by your Android and Google account settings, not by Clicksmith. Clicksmith does not receive those backups.

Children's privacy

Clicksmith is intended for a general audience, including children. Clicksmith does not collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Clicksmith's features or information practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
